D0.1% is a relative-volume surrogate, not an absolute Dmax or D0.03cc. HI = (D2% − D98%) / D50%, shown for target structures. Dmean integrates the supplied cumulative curve; truncated tails and missing volume metadata limit accuracy.

Research and educational use only. Not a validated treatment planning system, medical device, or substitute for independently verified clinical dose constraints.

Accepts a tabular cumulative DVH with Dose_Gy or Dose_cGy first, then one column per structure, strictly increasing dose from 0 Gy and non-increasing volumes from 0–100%. The file remains on your device; no patient data should be included in filenames or shared exports.
